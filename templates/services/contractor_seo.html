How long does contractor SEO take to work?
First ranking improvements typically appear in 60–90 days. Meaningful lead flow from SEO usually starts at 4–6 months. Compound growth kicks in at month 6 and beyond as your authority builds and more pages index. SEO is a long game — but the leads it generates are cheaper and convert better than paid traffic.
What's the difference between SEO for contractors and SEO for construction companies?
Contractor SEO emphasizes consumer-facing keywords like "plumber near me" and "HVAC repair [city]". Construction SEO emphasizes B2B terms like "commercial contractor [city]" and "tenant improvement contractor". Different keyword strategies, different content plans, different link sources. We specialize in both — see our General Contractor SEO page for the B2B angle.
Do you guarantee page 1 rankings?
No SEO agency can guarantee specific rankings — Google's algorithm changes constantly. What we guarantee: monthly measurable progress toward your target keywords, or we work for free the next month. You always know exactly where you stand with our plain-English monthly reports.
Can SEO replace Google Ads entirely?
Eventually, yes. Most of our clients scale back Google Ads 50–70% within 12 months of starting SEO. Organic leads convert at a higher rate and cost significantly less long-term — because once you own a ranking, you stop paying per click. We recommend running both in parallel during the first 6 months, then dialing back Ads as organic kicks in.
How much should I pay for contractor SEO?
Quality contractor SEO ranges from $800 to $3,000 per month depending on market competition and your service area size. Anything under $500/month is typically low-effort white-label work — recycled content, no real link building, no real results. We charge $899/month minimum because below that, we can't do the work that actually moves rankings.
Do I also need a new website for SEO to work?
If your site is older than 4 years or loads slowly on mobile, SEO will plateau. Google won't rank a slow, outdated site above a fast, modern competitor. We offer bundled pricing combining contractor website design and SEO at a discount — so you get the foundation and the rankings strategy in one package.
