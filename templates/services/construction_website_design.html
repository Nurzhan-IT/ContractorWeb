How do I build a construction website that wins commercial bids?
Focus on four things: a complete project portfolio with real metrics (square footage, timelines, cost ranges — not just photos), visible safety and certification credentials, a fast RFP form that asks the right qualifying questions, and page speed under 2 seconds on mobile. Project owners and GCs verify your track record before they send an RFP — make that verification effortless.
How to make a website for a construction company?
Start with what GCs and project owners want to verify: your track record, safety rating, insurance, and response time. Build every page around those trust signals. Then layer on lead-capture tools — RFP forms, project inquiry flows, and a service area map for commercial regions. Avoid generic contractor templates that bury your credentials in a footer.
Do you handle construction company website design ideas and inspiration?
Yes. Our discovery call includes a competitive analysis — we look at 3 to 5 construction sites in your market and identify winning patterns and gaps your competitors are missing. You come away from kickoff with a clear content strategy and design direction tailored to your specialty (commercial, residential, industrial, or specialty trades).
What about bidding platform integrations?
We integrate with Procore, BuildingConnected, and iSqFt lead feeds so that qualified bid opportunities from those platforms can flow directly to your team. We also build custom RFP intake forms that match the data fields required by municipal and commercial procurement portals.
Can I display ongoing projects without disclosing confidential details?
Yes. In-progress projects can be listed with project type, approximate size, and generic site photos while keeping client names, exact locations, and contract values private until project completion. We build a simple admin toggle that lets you control which details are visible on each project card.
