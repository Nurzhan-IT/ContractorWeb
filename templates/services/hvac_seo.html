What HVAC keywords should I target?
Start with high-intent service + location combos: "AC repair [city]", "HVAC contractor [city]", "emergency furnace repair [neighborhood]". Layer in seasonal searches: "AC tune-up [city]" in spring, "furnace installation [zip]" in fall. Then add long-tail problem queries: "AC not cooling", "heat pump blowing cold air in heat mode". Do I need city-level landing pages for HVAC SEO?
If you service more than one city, yes — city-level landing pages are one of the biggest lever moves for local HVAC SEO. Dedicated pages for each city you serve let Google understand your full service footprint and rank you accordingly.
