What's the difference between contractor lead generation services and HomeAdvisor/Angi?
Platforms like HomeAdvisor and Angi sell the same lead to 3–5 contractors simultaneously. By the time you call, the homeowner has already spoken to your competitors. Our leads are exclusive — one lead, one contractor, every time. Close rates on exclusive leads run 25–40% vs 8–12% for shared-lead platforms.
How is contractor lead gen different from SEO or paid ads?
Lead gen is the outcome; SEO and paid ads are channels. We combine Google Local Services Ads, Google Search ads, Facebook campaigns, and SEO funnels to deliver actual leads — not just rankings or impressions. You pay for qualified contact information, not clicks that may or may not convert.
Are the best contractor lead generation companies worth the cost?
It depends on the model. Shared-lead platforms: often no. Exclusive-lead agencies like ours: yes, if your close rate is above 20% and your average job value exceeds $500. Most trade contractors see ROI within 90 days. We show you the math before you commit.
What's a typical cost per lead for contractors?
Varies by trade and market. Plumbing: $30–$80. HVAC: $50–$120. Roofing storm restoration: $80–$200. General contractor commercial leads: $150–$400. These are exclusive leads — compare to $20–$60 for a shared lead you'll compete with 3–5 other contractors to close.
Can you do lead generation for painting, electrical, or independent contractors?
Yes. We generate leads for any home-services contractor: plumbing, HVAC, electrical, roofing, painting, fencing, landscaping, pest control, and more. The same multi-channel playbook adapts to any trade. We adjust targeting, qualification criteria, and CPL benchmarks by trade type.
Do you offer a lead generation platform or are you a service?
We are a full-service agency — we build, launch, and optimize your campaigns, and you take the calls. If you prefer a self-serve platform, tools like CallRail or Service Direct may fit better. If you want someone who runs the entire lead generation system for you, that's exactly what we do.
