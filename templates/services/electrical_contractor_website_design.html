What should an electrical contractor website include?
A high-converting electrical contractor website must display your licensing credentials and insurance prominently, list your full service menu (residential, commercial, industrial), include an instant quote tool for common jobs like panel upgrades, offer a 24/7 emergency request flow, show financing options for larger installs, feature a before/after photo gallery, and provide a service area map. Trust signals — master electrician status, state license number, manufacturer certifications — are especially important because electrical work carries safety implications that make homeowners cautious before calling.
Do you build websites for solar and EV charger specialists?
Yes. Any electrical sub-specialty gets dedicated landing pages optimized for those specific searches. If you install EV chargers, we build a dedicated EV charger installation page targeting searches like "Level 2 EV charger installation near me" and "home EV charging station cost." Same approach for solar inverter installs, whole-home backup generators, and industrial panel work. These pages rank independently and feed leads into a single dispatch flow.
Can the site show different pricing for residential vs commercial electrical work?
Yes. We use tab navigation or completely separate service pages, depending on how distinct your residential and commercial pricing and audiences are. Many electrical contractors maintain a residential section that ranks for homeowner searches and a commercial section targeting property managers and general contractors — both under the same domain with clean URL structures.
How important is mobile for electrical contractor websites?
Critical. 68% of electrical service searches happen on mobile. Emergency calls — tripping breaker, burning smell, power outage — are nearly 100% mobile. Our electrical contractor websites put "Call now" and "Emergency request" one tap away on every page, with tap-to-call phone numbers and streamlined mobile forms that don't require typing long addresses before someone calls you back.
Do you integrate with field service software for electrical contractors?
Yes. Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, and Jobber are all supported. Every form submission, online booking, and quote request flows directly into your dispatch software — no manual data entry. We also support Kickserv and FieldEdge for contractors already using those platforms.
What about SEO for electrical contractors?
On-page SEO fundamentals are included with every website build: meta tags, schema markup for ElectricalContractor, image optimization, and city-level landing pages for your service area.
