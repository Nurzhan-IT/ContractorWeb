What's different about general contractor website design vs regular contractor websites?
General contractors need to show licensing verifiably across states, demonstrate project management capability spanning multiple trades, and offer an RFP flow that handles complex scopes. Sub-contractor and trade-specialist sites have different priorities — they sell a single skill. GC websites sell the ability to orchestrate crews, subs, and timelines across multi-phase projects.
What are the best general contractor website examples?
Check our portfolio for live GC sites. Common winning traits: hero video of a completed project, visible and verifiable licensing, a simple RFP flow that qualifies scope upfront, and a mobile-first design that loads fast. The GC websites that convert best lead with proof — case studies, client testimonials, and specific project data like square footage, budget range, and timeline.
Do you have general contractor website templates?
We don't sell templates — every GC website is custom to your specific license territory, project mix, and bid patterns. How do I get the most leads from my GC website?
