{% endblock %} {#— FAQ —#} {% block faq_heading %}General Contractor SEO — Frequently Asked Questions{% endblock %} {% block service_faq %}
How is general contractor SEO different from regular contractor SEO?
General contractor SEO targets a completely different keyword universe. Consumer contractor SEO focuses on homeowner searches like "bathroom remodel near me". GC SEO targets B2B decision-makers: "commercial general contractor [city]", "tenant improvement contractor", "design-build construction management". The content strategy shifts from how-to guides to authority content that wins trust before an RFP goes out. Citation sources differ too — BuildingConnected and LoopNet instead of Angi and HomeAdvisor.
What keywords should a general contractor rank for?
Core commercial keywords include "commercial general contractor [city]", "tenant improvement contractor [metro]", "design-build contractor [state]", "construction management company [city]", and "commercial remodeling contractor". Beyond those, we target project-type keywords specific to your specialties — office buildout, warehouse construction, retail renovation, industrial GC — plus licensing keywords like "licensed bonded insured general contractor" that signal compliance to procurement teams.
Does general contractor SEO work if my leads come from relationships?
Yes. Even relationship-driven GCs see 20–40% of new pipeline from SEO once the site ranks — particularly from new market entrants: new property owners, architects expanding their network, or procurement teams vetting firms they haven't worked with before. SEO also reinforces relationships: when a referral Googles your company to validate before signing, a strong search presence closes the deal your relationship opened.
How long does it take to see ROI on general contractor SEO?
Expect 4–6 months for first qualified bids attributable to organic search, and 9–12 months for compound ROI where SEO becomes a consistent lead channel. B2B construction sales cycles are longer than consumer — a project owner who finds you in month 3 may not sign until month 8. We track the full pipeline, not just clicks, so you can see SEO's contribution to closed revenue.
What about Google Local Services Ads for general contractors?
Google LSAs work well for residential GCs but have limited reach for commercial. Commercial property owners and project managers don't use LSAs to source contractors — they search organically, check BuildingConnected profiles, review project portfolios, and verify licensing and safety records. For commercial GCs, SEO plus B2B citations plus case study content consistently outperforms paid ads in lead quality and cost per qualified bid.
Do I need a new website before starting general contractor SEO?
Not always. If your site is modern and includes a project portfolio, completed project descriptions, team bios, and licensing information, we can SEO-optimize it without a rebuild. If the site is dated, lacks mobile optimization, or is missing project case studies, start with a new site — it will accelerate SEO results significantly.
