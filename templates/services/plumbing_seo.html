How long does plumbing SEO take?
First rankings typically appear in 60–90 days. Meaningful lead flow starts at month 4–6. Emergency plumbing keywords can move faster because competition is often thinner outside the top-3 Map Pack. ROI compounds through year 2 as your domain authority grows.
What's the most important factor for plumbing SEO?
Google Business Profile. Roughly 80% of plumbing leads come from the Map Pack at the top of search results, not traditional organic listings. A fully optimized GBP — correct service categories, service area, photos, Q&A, and review volume — is the single biggest lever for a plumbing contractor's local SEO.
How many reviews do I need to rank?
Aim for 50+ reviews at 4.8+ average for most local plumbing markets. Competitive metros like Houston, LA, and NYC may require 200+ reviews to hold a top-3 Map Pack position. Our automated review acquisition system makes hitting these numbers realistic within the first 6 months.
Does plumbing SEO work for emergency-only plumbers?
Yes — especially. Emergency plumbing keywords like "burst pipe repair [city]", "24/7 plumber [city]", and "emergency water heater [city]" are ultra-high-intent searches where customers book within minutes of finding you. These keywords are a core part of every plan we run.
What about paid Google Ads for plumbing?
Paid ads and SEO work best together. Ads cover lead flow while your SEO ramps in months 1–6, then SEO handles volume at a much lower cost per lead. Plumbing Ads clicks run $20–40 each — once your organic rankings are established, those clicks are free.
Do I need a new plumbing website first?
Our free audit tells you. If your existing site has solid structure, decent page speed, and is mobile-friendly, we work with it. If it is on an outdated CMS, loads slowly on mobile, or has poor on-page architecture, our plumbing website design service builds a new site built to rank.
