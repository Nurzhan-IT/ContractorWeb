How is construction lead generation different from general contractor lead generation?
Construction lead generation targets commercial and B2B buyers — property managers, developers, architects, facility directors, REITs. General contractor lead generation often includes residential homeowners. Different audiences, different channels, different messaging. Commercial is LinkedIn ABM and trade-publication presence; residential is Google LSA and Facebook. Same word "lead gen", completely different playbook.
What are the best construction lead generation companies?
Niche agencies that understand B2B construction sales cycles outperform generalist lead gen firms every time. Ask three things before hiring: do they use LinkedIn ABM? Do they read ENR and Construction Dive? Do they understand that your sales cycle runs 3–12 months and optimize attribution for that? If all three answers are yes, they can help. If the pitch sounds like residential tactics repackaged, walk away.
Do you offer construction lead generation platforms or just services?
We are not a monthly retainer agency, and we are not a self-serve platform. We build the full B2B lead generation system into your $1,499 one-time website (LinkedIn ad accounts, Google Ads, RFP-intake forms, ABM templates, CRM integration). You own everything after launch and run campaigns yourself. If you want a self-service shared-lead platform instead, BuildingConnected and iSqFt are the standard options — but those deliver shared or auction-style leads (the same RFP goes to 5–20 bidders). Our inbound leads are exclusive to you.
How does the pricing work — is there a monthly fee?
The full B2B construction lead generation system — LinkedIn ad accounts, Google Ads setup, RFP intake forms, landing pages, case study templates, CRM integration, conversion tracking — is built into our $1,499 one-time website package. No monthly retainer to us. The only ongoing cost is your ad spend on LinkedIn and Google, paid directly to those platforms. You own the accounts and the funnels.
What is a realistic cost per lead for commercial construction?
$200–$800 per qualified RFP-ready lead in ad spend (paid directly to LinkedIn and Google, not to us). Is that higher than residential CPA? Yes. Is the LTV higher? Dramatically — 100 to 500 times higher. Commercial lead economics only make sense at the pipeline level, not the per-lead level. The lead dashboard built into the site tracks pipeline value and close-rate velocity, not just volume.
Do I need to also do construction SEO?
SEO and lead generation together outperform either alone. Qualified inbound leads from SEO cost less over time and close at higher rates because the buyer came to you. Paid lead generation fills the pipeline while SEO ramps up over the first 6–9 months. Most of our best construction clients run both — paid for immediate pipeline, SEO for compounding inbound flow.
