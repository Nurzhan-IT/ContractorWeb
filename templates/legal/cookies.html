Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Good news: this site uses only strictly necessary cookies. We do not use any analytics, advertising, tracking, or marketing cookies.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function correctly and to provide information to site owners.

2. Cookies We Use

Cookie Name Category Purpose Duration
csrftoken Strictly Necessary Protects all forms from Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks. Required for the site to function securely. 1 year
sessionid Strictly Necessary Maintains your session state while you navigate between pages. Does not contain any personal information. Session (deleted when browser closes)
cookie_notice_seen Strictly Necessary Stored in localStorage (not a cookie) — remembers that you dismissed the cookie notice so it is not shown again. Persistent (localStorage)

3. What We Do NOT Use

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics, Yandex Metrica, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • Advertising or retargeting cookies (Meta Pixel, Google Ads, etc.)
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Any third-party tracking or profiling cookies

4. Third-Party Scripts

Some pages load scripts from third parties that may set their own cookies or receive your IP address:

Service Pages What it receives
Cloudflare Turnstile Quote, Web Quote forms User interaction signals to detect bots. May set its own functional cookies.
Cal.com Embed Booking page only Booking data you enter (name, email, time slot). Cal.com may set session cookies.
Tailwind CSS CDN All pages Stylesheet delivery only. No known tracking.

5. How to Control Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling the csrftoken cookie will prevent forms from working correctly.

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
